Prithvi (he/him) is a recent graduate from NYU's Archives and Public History program. He is a trained archivist with experience in digitization, preservation, and processing. His interest lie in digital archives & collections, specifically in identifying methods of ensuring access and discoverability for *all* without sacrificing context and nuance. 

Born and raised in Singapore, Prithvi arrived in the United States in 2019 to pursue a B.A. from Occidental College in Los Angeles, and graduated in 2023 with a double-major in History and Diplomacy & World Affairs. Since then, he was worked across a myriad of instiutitons and repositories including New York Historical, LACMA, NYU Libraries, and NHMLAC. He is currently based in NYC and open to work. 

When he is not wading around in metadata, Prithvi volunteers at [XFR Collective](https://xfrcollective.org/), a non-profit organization that works towards lowering the barriers to preserving at-risk audiovisual media. In his free time, you can find him bouldering at the local climbing gym, playing football (as in futbol), or hanging out with his cat, Dirt.
